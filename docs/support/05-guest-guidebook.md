# How the Guest Guidebook Works

The FieldStay Guest Guidebook delivers your guests their door code, WiFi password,
and local recommendations via a personalized link — and turns local business
relationships into a revenue stream.

## What the Guest Experiences
Every guest gets a pre-arrival email with a link to their personalized guidebook:
door access code, WiFi name and password, check-in instructions, house rules,
and local recommendations from sponsored businesses.

## SMS Opt-In
The pre-arrival email prompts guests to enter their phone number for a door code
text message. Opted-in guests receive one check-in SMS with door code, WiFi, and
portal link. During the stay they may receive one morning and one evening message
with a sponsor recommendation, a featured-amenity note, or both — see **Contextual
SMS Recommendations** below for what drives these. All messages include STOP.

## Setting Up the Guidebook
After your PMS sync, each property gets a guidebook pre-populated with WiFi and
check-in instructions. Review at Guidebook → [Property Name] and toggle Published
when ready. Guests cannot see it until it is published.

## The Sponsor Model
Local businesses pay $15/month per featured slot, and $5 of that comes back to you.
- Every active sponsor: $5/month plan credit, from the first one — no thresholds
- 3 active sponsors: the Guidebook feature itself is permanently unlocked (free). Separate from the credit.

Go to Guidebook → Sponsors → Add Sponsor to add a business. A media kit link is
auto-generated to share with potential sponsors.

## Gap Night Messaging
When a guest is near checkout and there are open nights after their booking,
FieldStay can send an offer to extend their stay. Configure under Guidebook →
Gap Night Settings. Off by default.

## Contextual SMS Recommendations
Morning and evening messages combine two independent sources, either of which is
enough to trigger a send:

- **Featured amenities** — go to Guidebook → [Property Name] and pick up to 3
  amenities to feature (checkboxes pulled from what your PMS synced for that
  property), then write a short guest-facing note for each, semicolon-separated
  in the order you checked them — e.g. "Takes 45 min to heat.; Starter logs on
  the back porch." Semicolons (not commas) separate the notes, since a note
  itself can reasonably contain a comma. Messages rotate through your featured
  amenities across a guest's stay so they don't see the same one every day. If
  you don't pick any, FieldStay features the first 3 amenities your PMS synced
  automatically.
- **Sponsor recommendations** — driven by live weather (a rainy-day sponsor when
  it's raining, a morning coffee or dinner sponsor otherwise) and proximity, as
  described in **Setting Up Sponsors for Your Guidebook**.

A property with only featured amenities and no active sponsors still sends
messages; a property with only sponsors and no featured amenities does too. When
both are configured, a single message includes both.
