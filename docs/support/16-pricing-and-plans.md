# FieldStay Pricing and Plans

FieldStay is priced by the number of properties you manage, with ONE graduated (marginal) rate schedule — there are no discrete plan tiers to pick between, and no tier-to-tier feature differences. Every subscription includes the full platform. (The one exception is the Guest Guidebook, which has its own separate unlock condition — see below.)

---

## How Pricing Works

Your bill is computed property-by-property, not by which "tier" you're in:

| Properties | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1st property | $49/mo flat |
| 2nd–4th property | $13/mo each |
| 5th–15th property | $10/mo each |
| 16th–50th property | $8/mo each |
| 51st–150th property | $6/mo each |

This is graduated pricing: crossing from, say, 4 properties to 5 only changes what the 5th property itself costs ($10) — it never re-rates the properties you already had. There is no "plan boundary" where your whole bill jumps.

Some worked examples (monthly):

| Properties | Total/month |
|---|---|
| 1 | $49 |
| 4 | $88 |
| 5 | $98 |
| 15 | $198 |
| 50 | $478 |
| 100 | $778 |
| 150 | $1,078 |

Settings → Billing shows this same math for your account, itemized line by line, at your current property count.

Properties beyond 150 are Enterprise territory — email hello@fieldstay.app for custom pricing. There is no self-serve checkout above 150 properties.

## What Every Subscription Includes

There are no plan-tier feature gates — every FieldStay subscription includes the complete platform:

- Turnover management and crew scheduling
- Work orders and vendor management
- Inventory tracking and Kroger cart building
- Asset health tracking and depreciation reporting
- Owner portal and financial ledger
- OwnerRez, Hospitable, and Hostex integrations (Hostaway integration in progress)
- RepuGuard AI review response drafting
- Guest Guidebook with SMS delivery — unlocked during your trial, and
  after that once you have 3 active sponsors, staying unlocked for as long
  as you keep at least 3 (see **Guidebook Sponsor Credits** below); it isn't
  included by default
- Crew mobile PWA (offline-capable)

The one feature with its own unlock condition is the Guest Guidebook, described above.

## Annual Billing

Annual billing is available at a discount equivalent to 2 months free — roughly 17% off the monthly rate (every rate in the schedule above, and your total, is simply 10x the monthly figure). Annual plans are billed once per year upfront.

To switch to annual billing, go to Settings → Billing or contact support@fieldstay.app.

---

## Guidebook Sponsor Credits

The Guest Guidebook includes a sponsor model where local businesses pay $15/month to be featured:

- 3 active sponsors → the Guidebook feature itself is permanently unlocked (free) — this is separate from your FieldStay subscription
- 5 active sponsors → $10/month credit applied to your next bill
- 6 active sponsors → $25/month credit applied to your next bill (doesn't stack with the 5-sponsor credit)

Credits apply automatically each billing cycle based on your active sponsor count.

## Adding or Removing Properties

There's no plan cap to hit and no "upgrade" button — add a property and your bill will reflect it, automatically, at exactly that property's rate on the schedule above. When it actually shows up on an invoice depends on your billing interval:

- **Monthly billing**: an added or removed property is reflected starting your NEXT invoice. Nothing changes mid-cycle, and there's nothing to click.
- **Annual billing**: added properties are held rather than billed individually as you add them. Once you've added a 5th property since your last renewal, all 5 are prorated together for the remainder of your billing year in one adjustment — not backdated, starting from that day forward. Removed properties are credited at your next renewal.

Self-serve billing covers up to 150 properties. Beyond that, contact hello@fieldstay.app.

## Billing and Invoices

FieldStay billing is managed through Stripe. Go to **Settings → Billing** and click **Manage Billing** — this opens Stripe's hosted billing portal, where you can update your payment method and download past invoices. There's no separate in-app payment-method page; it all lives inside that Stripe portal.

Invoices are sent to the email address on your FieldStay account on each billing date.

---

## Cancelling Your Subscription

To cancel, go to **Settings → Billing**, click **Manage Billing** to open Stripe's hosted portal, and cancel from there — or email support@fieldstay.app and we'll do it for you. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation.
