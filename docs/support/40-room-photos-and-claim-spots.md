# Room Photos and Claim Spots

Every room section on a turnover checklist ends with one required photo: a wide shot of the whole room from the doorway. Alongside it, each property has a short list of claim spots, the places that tend to show up in damage claims, and each of those gets a required photo too. Together they give you a consistent before and after of every room on every stay.

Your crew sees these as ordinary checklist items with a camera icon. The checklist will not complete until each one has a photo.

---

## The room photo

At the end of every room section (Kitchen, Master Bedroom, Bathroom, and so on) there is one item: **Room condition photo: stand in the doorway and photograph the whole room.** Crew tap it, take the photo, and it is sealed and filed to the stay.

Sections that are not rooms do not get one. Asset Discovery, Exterior, Arrival, Departure, Final Checks, Supplies, and Inventory are skipped. If you named a section something unusual and it is getting a room photo it should not, rename the section in the template or contact support.

The room photo is the one that pairs across stays. A claim packet puts the Kitchen photo from before a guest beside the Kitchen photo from after them, by section name. Keep section names stable in your templates so the pairs line up.

---

## Claim spots

Claim spots are the specific places in a home that matter when something goes wrong. FieldStay starts every property with a default list the first time a turnover is generated:

| Room | Spot |
| --- | --- |
| Bedrooms | Mattress, cover pulled back |
| Living room | Sofa cushions, lifted |
| Dining room | Table top |
| Kitchen | Oven interior, door open |
| Kitchen | Refrigerator interior |
| Bathrooms | Shower and tub from the door |

Each spot becomes a required photo item in its section on every turnover.

### Editing the list

Open the property, click **Evidence**, and find the **Claim photo spots** card. From there you can:

- **Add a spot.** Pick the section (room) and type a short label. Keep it under a few words. The label is what your crew reads.
- **Edit a label.**
- **Remove a spot.** The spot stops appearing on new turnovers. Photos already taken for it stay on the record.

Changes apply to the next turnover FieldStay generates, not to checklists already open.

### How many to keep

Five to eight photos per home, counting the room photos, is the right number. That is about 90 seconds of extra work on a turnover. A list of twenty gets skipped, and a skipped photo is worth exactly as much as no system.

If a spot's section no longer exists on the template, the spot appears in its own **Evidence photos** section with the old room name in front, so nothing is silently dropped.

## What your crew sees

The items look like any other checklist item that needs a photo, with the reason **Before and after record for this stay** under the task. Crew using the app in Spanish see the task and reason in Spanish.

The confirm button at the bottom of the checklist stays disabled, with a count of photos still required, until every photo item has one.

---

## Common questions

**My crew says this is too many photos.**
Trim the claim spots. Keep the room photos. One doorway shot per room is the minimum that makes a before and after possible.

**Can I turn the room photo off for one property?**
Not today. You can remove every claim spot, which leaves only the room photos.

**A room photo was taken from the wrong angle. Can crew retake it?**
Yes. Tapping the item again replaces the photo on the checklist. Both photos stay on the evidence record.
