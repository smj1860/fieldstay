# Claim Countdown

When a stay has a reason to file a damage claim, FieldStay counts down the days you have left to file it and tells you in the notification bell. The point is simple: most claims are lost to the deadline, not to the evidence.

---

## What starts a countdown

A countdown opens when either of these happens on a turnover:

- You or a team member **flag** the turnover from the turnovers board
- Your crew **reports an issue** from the crew app, which creates a work order

Nothing starts a countdown on its own. A normal stay with normal photos never produces one.

---

## What you get

Four notifications over the life of the window, each at most once:

| When | Notification |
| --- | --- |
| The countdown opens | Days left to file a claim at the property, with the date |
| Seven days left | Claim deadline in 7 days, with a prompt to build the packet |
| Two days left | Claim deadline in 2 days |
| The window closes | Claim window closed, with the date it ended |

Each one links to the property's Evidence page with that stay selected, so building the packet is one click away.

A window that closed more than a few days ago does not produce a closed notice. Old news is not news.

---

## How the deadline is set

The deadline depends on where the booking came from:

- **Airbnb and VRBO stays** count 14 days after checkout, and the notification says so. The window is the same whether or not another guest checks in right after. Platform rules change, and the platform's own help center is the authority. If in doubt, file early.
- **Direct bookings and other sources** have no platform deadline. FieldStay uses your own **Claim window**, in days after checkout. Set it on any property's Evidence page in the **Claim window** card. The default is 14 days, and it applies to your whole account.

---

## Common questions

**I flagged a turnover by mistake. Can I stop the countdown?**
Unflag the turnover. Notifications already sent stay in the bell; no further ones arrive.

**Why did I get a closed notice for a stay from last month?**
You should not have. If a stay was flagged after its window had already closed by more than a few days, no notice is sent. If you see one, contact support with the property and stay dates.

**Does the countdown file anything for me?**
No. It tells you the date. Filing is yours.
